Evidence-first BaZi research prototype

Test uncertainty before interpretation.

Recorded birth time is evidence, not ground truth. This prototype keeps chart derivation and rectification deterministic, gives people control over context, and permits AI only after the upstream evidence is fixed.

Why the boundary matters

One pipeline, three explicit responsibilities.

01

Deterministic upstream

Candidate generation, event backtesting, and rectification stay in auditable code. AI cannot alter the selected chart.

02

User-controlled context

Context can be hidden from forecasts, excluded from exports, or deleted. It never leaks back into chart ranking.

03

Measured downstream

Offline A/B/C/D evaluation separates derivative-only, context-only, default-chart, and selected-chart modes.
