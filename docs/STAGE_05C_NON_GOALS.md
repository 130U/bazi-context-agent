# Stage 5C Non-Goals

Stage 5C must not do the following:

1. Do not implement Rectification v2 scoring.
2. Do not choose the correct chart.
3. Do not generate final forecast.
4. Do not call AI for chart generation.
5. Do not let context_box affect chart generation.
6. Do not let context_box affect ranking.
7. Do not modify `/api/ranking` scoring.
8. Do not modify Stage 4 prediction provider policy.
9. Do not create real `.env` files.
10. Do not write real API keys.
11. Do not add login, payment, database, or user system.
12. Do not implement Zi Wei Dou Shu, Qi Men Dun Jia, Feng Shui, or other systems.
13. Do not implement full rectification event scoring. That is Stage 5D.
14. Do not implement ForecastInput Builder. That is Stage 5E.
