# Privacy / 隐私

## Principles

- Local-first by default.
- No account or cloud sync by default.
- Export/import are user-controlled.
- Users can hide or delete facts.
- Reports and exports must apply redaction.

## Sensitive data

The system may handle family background, education, career, relationship, health-year events, migration, preferences, and current concerns. Users should avoid entering information they do not want to store locally or export.

## Secrets

Do not include API keys, tokens, `.env` content, or local paths in reports or exports.
